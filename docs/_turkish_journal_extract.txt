Thank you for submitting your manuscript to the Turkish Journal of Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences (TJEECS). The editors of the Turkish Journal of Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences wish to accept only papers that, in addition to being scientifically sound, not very specific, not misleading, and not previously published, satisfy also the following criteria:-- The research must have been performed to a technical standard high enough to allow robust conclusions to be drawn from the data/results. Methods must be described in sufficient detail so that another researcher is able to reproduce the findings described.-- The results must be interpreted appropriately, such that all conclusions are justified. However, authors may discuss possible explanations for their results as long as these are clearly identified as speculations or hypotheses, rather than as firm conclusions. Inappropriate interpretation of results is unacceptable.— The novelty and the contribution of the research to the field should be emphasized in the manuscript in a very clear way by referring the recent literature of the research line.— The research must attract attention of broadest possible section of our readership and must represent a relatively significant breakthrough of general interest in the research field.-- Perfect, stylish English is not essential but the language must be clear and unambiguous. If the language of a paper is poor, the authors may consider seeking independent help before submission. Poor presentation and/or language is often a reason for return and rejection.Our subject editors who are expert in your research field find that your manuscript does not satisfy one or several of these criteria and recommend us not to start evaluation process. We feel that it is the right way to proceed - allowing you to move on without delay. Consequently, this is not a rejection and you should not consider it as such.

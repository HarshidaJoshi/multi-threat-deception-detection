XAI-Verify is a research prototype that combines deep learning detection with explainable AI (Grad-CAM for images, exact word contributions for text) for transparent decision support.

Research Modules

  • DeepFake Image Detection — ResNet50 features with a trained MLP head on 100,000 real vs. AI-generated face images (95.4% held-out accuracy), with Grad-CAM heatmaps computed from the model showing where it looked.
  • Misinformation Detection — Text classifiers trained on WELFake (fake news) and EMSCAD (recruitment scams), with exact word contributions from the linear models.
    • Fake News — news articles and social posts
    • Recruitment Scam — fraudulent job postings: a classifier trained on EMSCAD plus employer and website checks
  • Explainable AI — Grad-CAM for images; for text, each word's exact contribution to the linear model's score, shown with every result.

How to Check a Government Website

For recruitment scam detection, users can check whether a job website looks like an official government site or a fake one. This project uses simple domain rules — it does not connect to India.gov.in automatically. Users should verify jobs on official portals themselves.

How to check (simple steps):

  1. Look at the website address. Real Indian government sites usually end with .gov.in, .nic.in, or .ac.in (for universities).
  2. Check that the link starts with https:// (secure connection).
  3. Open India.gov.in, NCS.gov.in, or Employment News and search for the same job or department.
  4. Be careful if the job asks for registration fee, interview fee, or payment before joining. Real government jobs rarely ask for money on unofficial websites.
Website type Government registered? Example
.gov.in / .nic.in Yes — government pattern employmentnews.gov.in
.ac.in (university) Yes — government/education pattern iitb.ac.in
Private company (official) No — but can be real (not government) infosys.com, tcs.com
.xyz, .top, .tk No — suspicious, often used in scams railway-jobs.xyz
Unknown website Cannot confirm — check on India.gov.in Verify manually

Official links for manual verification: India.gov.in · NCS.gov.in · Employment News
